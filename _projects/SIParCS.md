I completed my first internship at NCAR (the National Center for Atmospheric Research) in Boulder, Colorado in the summer of 2023. I worked to explore the feasibility of using ElasticSearch to index a 90 Pebibyte repository of scientific data.

I worked at the Mesa Laboratory, a building designed by I. M. Pei to blend into the The Flatirons. There are plenty of photos of the building online. Below is a photo of the view from my office.

<img src="/images/project/SIParCS/office-view.jpg" width="500" />

As a part of the internship, I took a trip to see NCAR's super computer in Cheyenne, Wyoming. They provided a tour that included the new super computer <i>Derecho</i> as well as the old super computer pictured below. 


<img src="/images/project/SIParCS/SuperComputer_Photo.JPEG" width="500" />


I also presented my poster to the employees, scientists, and community surrounding NCAR. 

<img src="/images/project/SIParCS/poster.jpg" width="500" />

After completing this internship I won the UW-Stout 2024 Student Internship of the Year Award.
