As required by the UW-Stout Honor's College, I sought out to spend 60+ hours on an individual learning opportunity.

This led to over 95 hours of learning, mistakes, and mathematics compacted into a handmade, crochet blanket.

With my basic knitting and sewing skills, I took on the challenge of learning to crochet. For my final creation, I set out to make a small blanket. I initially followed a basic pattern and then adapted to the challenges that came along the way. Alongside the creation of a blanket, I kept a time log with row counts and calculated averages to be able to explore the mathematics of crochet even further. You can read all about that experience in my paper <i>Exploring Mathematical Concepts Through Crochet</i> here ...
