I am a backend-software developer at IBM and graduated in May 2025 with a Bachelor of Science from the University of Wisconsin-Stout. I double majored in Computer Science as well as Applied Mathematics and Computer Science (AMCS) with a concentration in Software Development. 

I was the President of the AMCS Women’s Group, a Student Representative for the AMCS Program Advisory Committee, and a Student Instructor for Operating Systems, and a Student Office Assistant in the Mathematics, Statistics, and Computer Science Department. I was also an active member of UW-Stout’s Honors College and earned Summa Cum Laude honors at graduation.

While at Stout, I partook in two summer internships. The first was at the National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR). For this internship, I created a program to test the feasibility of indexing metadata on a mid-sized repository for the purpose of increasing access to scientific data and research. The second was at Mid-States Concrete Industries where I worked on smaller projects throughout their many departments. These projects included building a scanning inventory system, creating a personalized rebar calculator, and more.
