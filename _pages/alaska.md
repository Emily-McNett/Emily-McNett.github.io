Day 2: May 15, 2022
We went out on a small hike to see a glacier and 3/5 ended up waist deep in some snow - took some pictures but the snow is very bright so I tried my best not to squint in everyone... Then we went to a fishing lookout and I took some pictures with the built in binoculars they had. We also visited an animal reserve where there were 3 moose, some brown bears, a black bear, and a lot of other animals. Kal got some wood bison fur because one of them rubbed against the fence. We wrapped up the day with going to see another bor tide, having a very interesting dinner at a place called Chair 5, and going for a swim in the resorts pool :) love you!! Good night - this 3 hour time difference doesn't help anybody
